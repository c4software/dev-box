Backing the box up and restoring it, from the host

  The home, the projects and the Tailscale state live in ./data on the
  host: a rebuild loses nothing, a dead disk loses it all. Both scripts run
  on the host, in the install directory, and need zstd there.

  1. On the host: write an archive
       scripts/backup.sh             into ./backups
       scripts/backup.sh /mnt/disk   into another directory
     It holds the home without its caches, the projects without their
     node_modules, .env and compose.override.yaml. .env carries TS_AUTHKEY
     and GITHUB_TOKEN: keep the archive as a secret.

  2. On the host: put it somewhere else
     An archive left on the same disk protects from a mistake, not from the
     disk. Copy it to another machine.

  3. On the host: restore
       scripts/restore.sh backups/dev-box-<name>-<date>.tar.zst
     It stops the container, lists what would be overwritten and asks
     before unpacking. Nothing outside the archive is deleted. Then:
       docker compose up -d

  4. In the box: the tools
     The mise toolchains were not in the archive: the start installs them
     again, or
       devbox update tools

  What stays out
     data/tailscale, the identity of the node: restored elsewhere, two
     machines would claim the same one. scripts/backup.sh --with-tailscale
     takes it in. The podman images are left out too, and are pulled again.

  Sources
     docs/backup.md of the repository:
       https://github.com/c4software/dev-box/blob/main/docs/backup.md
