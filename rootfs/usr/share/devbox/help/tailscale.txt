From plain SSH to Tailscale: the box on your tailnet

  A box started with TS_DISABLE=true is reached through its own SSH server,
  on a port of the host. With Tailscale it becomes a machine of your tailnet
  instead: reached from anywhere by its name, nothing published on the
  host, and devbox serve, Taildrop and Taildrive at hand. The two never run
  together. Headscale works too.

  1. On the host: the settings
     In the install directory, in .env:
       TS_DISABLE=false
       TS_HOSTNAME=dev-box   the name of the machine on the tailnet
     Optional: TS_AUTHKEY, to attach without a login URL, and
     TS_LOGIN_SERVER, the URL of your Headscale.

  2. On the host: restart, and attach the box
       docker compose up -d
       docker compose logs -f
     The logs print a login URL: open it, or give it to headscale nodes
     register. With TS_AUTHKEY the box attaches on its own. The identity of
     the node is kept in ./data/tailscale: this happens once.

  3. In the policy of your tailnet: allow the shell
     Tailscale SSH is refused without an ssh rule, and that rule alone does
     not open the network: once the policy has grants or acls, traffic to
     the box must be allowed too, or port 22 is filtered.
       "grants": [{ "src": ["alice@"], "dst": ["alice@"], "ip": ["*"] }],
       "ssh": [{ "action": "accept", "src": ["alice@"],
                 "dst": ["alice@"], "users": ["dev"] }]
     src and dst: the user who owns the machines. users: your account in
     the box (USER_NAME).

  4. On your machine: connect
       ssh dev@dev-box
     From any machine of the tailnet. On the host, this always works:
       docker exec -it -u dev dev-box zsh -l

  5. In the box: what it opens
       devbox serve 3000         a dev server, published on the tailnet
       devbox tailscale send     a file to another machine, over Taildrop
       devbox tailscale share    this folder, over Taildrive
       devbox status             says how the box is reached

  Going back
     TS_DISABLE=true and SSH_AUTHORIZED_KEYS in .env, then docker compose
     up -d: the box starts its own SSH server again.

  Sources
     docs/access.md of the repository, where the policy was validated with
     Headscale:
       https://github.com/c4software/dev-box/blob/main/docs/access.md
