An iPhone or iPad plugged into your machine, the box on another

  The USB block of compose.override.example.yaml only reaches a device that
  is plugged into the machine running the box. When the box is on a remote
  machine and the device on your desk, the usbmuxd of your own machine does
  the USB part and the box talks to it through the network. Nothing goes
  into the compose file, no usbmuxd runs in the box, and the pairing records
  stay on your machine: a rebuild of the box asks for no new Trust.

  1. On your machine: the device and its usbmuxd
     Linux: install usbmuxd (Arch: sudo pacman -S usbmuxd), which starts on
     its own when a device is plugged in. macOS has it already. Plug the
     iPhone or iPad in, unlock it, answer Trust.

  2. On your machine: connect to the box with usbmuxd in tow
       ssh -R 27015:/var/run/usbmuxd <user>@<box>
     Port 27015 of the box, on its loopback only, leads to the usbmuxd of
     your machine for as long as that SSH session lasts. From Windows, where
     Apple's service listens on a port: ssh -R 27015:127.0.0.1:27015.

  3. In the box, in that session: point xtool at it
       export USBMUXD_SOCKET_ADDRESS=127.0.0.1:27015
       xtool devices
       xtool dev
     xtool reads that variable, its own Docker image is built on it.
     pymobiledevice3 takes the address as an option:
       pymobiledevice3 usbmux list --usbmux 127.0.0.1:27015
     Put the export line in your shell config to keep it.

  When the SSH connection refuses the forward
     Both machines are on the tailnet: let your machine listen there instead
     (socat is a package of every distribution), and point the box at it.
       socat TCP-LISTEN:27015,bind=<its tailnet address>,reuseaddr,fork \
         UNIX-CONNECT:/var/run/usbmuxd
     and in the box:
       export USBMUXD_SOCKET_ADDRESS=<your machine>:27015
     Whoever your tailnet policy lets reach that port can drive the device:
     stop socat when you are done.

  The other ways
     The device can go to the machine running the box: plug it there, then
     step 2 of `devbox help ios`.
     Without a cable, over Tailscale, once paired over USB: not something the
     box sets up (iOS 27, a TUN device, a Python the box does not have), and
     xtool does not go through it. The route is written down in
       https://github.com/joshuaswarren/omarchy-apple-dev/blob/main/docs/OVER-TAILNET.md

  Sources
     Step 3 comes from the Dockerfile of xtool:
