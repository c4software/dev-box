An iPhone or iPad over Wi-Fi, without the cable

  iOS 27 only: earlier versions accept no new computer over the network.
  The device is paired once over USB, then devbox ios xcode installs,
  launches and reads the logs over Wi-Fi, through the RemotePairing tunnel
  of omarchy-apple-dev (tools/tailnet-tunnel.py). It needs the sign-in with
  an App Store Connect API key (mode 0 of devbox ios): signed in with an
  Apple ID password, a Flutter app, or device-run.sh without
  --over-tailnet, the cable stays.
  mDNS does not reach the box, so the device is given by its address.

  1. Once, with the device on USB
     The device of step 2 of `devbox help ios`, which gives the box the USB
     bus of the host in compose.override.yaml (or of `devbox help ios-usb`),
     Developer Mode on, then:
       devbox ios pair
     It starts the usbmuxd of the box itself, as the USB commands do.
     No Trust this time: the device trusts the box already. The record
     lands in ~/.local/share/pymobiledevice3/remote_<udid>.plist, in the
     home, so a rebuild keeps it. It holds a private key: whoever has it and
     reaches the device drives it, keep it like an SSH key.

  2. On the device: Wi-Fi and an address the box reaches
     Wi-Fi on, whatever the network: on cellular only the device closes the
     port. The simplest address is its tailnet one, with the Tailscale app
     on and signed in to the tailnet of the box; it works wherever the box
     and the device are:
       tailscale status      in the box, the iOS line
     With Tailscale off in the box (TS_DISABLE=true), the Tailscale of the
     host routes that address as well: measured from rootless Docker on its
     default bridge, iOS 27.2.
     The address of the device on your local network also works when the
     box routes to it (the box on a machine of that network), which
     omarchy-apple-dev has not measured yet. Unlock it once after a restart.

  3. In the box: the app over Wi-Fi
       cd ~/projets/<app>
       devbox ios xcode --wifi 100.x.y.z           build, install, launch
       devbox ios xcode logs --wifi 100.x.y.z
       export IOS_WIFI=100.x.y.z                   the same without --wifi
     The command opens the tunnel, as root through sudo for its TUN device
     (/dev/net/tun, given to the box by compose.yaml), and closes it when it
     ends. A box started with docker run rather than compose.yaml needs them
     on its command line, as compose.yaml has them for tailscaled:
       --device /dev/net/tun --cap-add NET_ADMIN --cap-add NET_RAW
     (measured with both; whether NET_RAW is needed was not tried).
     Several devices paired: IOS_UDID=<udid> picks one. An xtool
     package without an Xcode project goes through omarchy-apple-dev,
     with an .ipa signed already (tools/sign-dev.sh of the clone):
       device-run.sh --over-tailnet 100.x.y.z <App>.ipa

  When it fails
     Connection refused on every port: Wi-Fi off on the device, or
     Tailscale off (tailscale ping <address> from the box). The connection
     cut right after it opens: the record does not match, devbox ios pair
     again on USB, also after a reset of the device. The app does not launch
     on a device left asleep for hours: wake it to its lock screen first.

  Debugging
     lldb over Wi-Fi is measured upstream but not wrapped by a command yet:
     device-run.sh --lldb needs the cable. The manual route, with its
     sysroot and its long first stop, is in LLDB over the tunnel of
       https://github.com/joshuaswarren/omarchy-apple-dev/blob/main/docs/OVER-TAILNET.md

  Sources
     The route, its limits and the measures (iOS 27.0.1) come from
